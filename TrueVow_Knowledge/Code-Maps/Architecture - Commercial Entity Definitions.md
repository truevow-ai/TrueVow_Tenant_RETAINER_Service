# Commercial Entity Definitions

Trial intake = completed Benjamin intake session reaching defined completion point. NOT raw inbound calls, spam, or hang-ups. Trial meter consumes 1 per completed intake. Monthly call allowances (40/100/200) apply after paid activation. Billing and INTAKE must share exact canonical definition before commissioning trial meter.
