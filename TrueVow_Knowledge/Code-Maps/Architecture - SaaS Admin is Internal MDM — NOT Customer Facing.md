# SaaS Admin is Internal MDM — NOT Customer Facing

SaaS Administration Service is the Master Data Management (MDM) system for ALL internal services. It is accessed ONLY by management and the CSM team. It is NOT a customer-facing service. It provisions tenants, manages platform configuration, and serves as the administrative backbone.
