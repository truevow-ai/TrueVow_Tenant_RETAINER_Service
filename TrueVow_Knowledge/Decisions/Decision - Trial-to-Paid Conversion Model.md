# Trial-to-Paid Conversion Model

Canonical commercial lifecycle: 90-day/12-intake trial auto-activates after onboarding. Firm may select paid plan at any time during trial but paid plan does NOT activate until trial exhaustion (12th intake OR day 90). Three distinct dates: plan_selected_at, trial_ends_at, paid_subscription_activated_at. No service interruption at conversion. Trial entitlement is THE authoritative entitlement until trial ends. Optional 'start now' for immediate activation but not the default path. Intake = completed Benjamin intake session, not raw inbound call.
